Door schedule

New work from an export waits in Holding, soonest due date first — drag it onto a day to schedule it. Days only show work someone has actually committed to. EFFICIENCY.

Earned hours are the estimated time of whatever was ticked off that day. Actual hours default to what the day was given — type over them with what was really worked. Efficiency is earned ÷ actual; plan attainment is earned ÷ planned.

DAILY DETAIL.

WORKING HOURS.

Hours available at each step, per weekday. The boards measure a day's work against these, and a day at zero is closed.

SPECIFIC DAYS.

Overtime, shutdowns and public holidays. These beat the weekly pattern above. Queensland public holidays are loaded at zero hours — edit or remove any that don't apply.

MINUTES PER UNIT.

How long one unit takes at each step. A product's own figure wins; otherwise the default row; otherwise BC's estimate, shown in grey under each box. Products no longer in the exports are kept and marked.

POWDER COLOURS.

Colours set by hand from a tile on the powdercoat schedule. These beat the colour charts. Remove one to go back to the chart's colour.
